# Peak Unemployment brand

Always write the full name **Peak Unemployment**. “After Hours” names the product experience, not a replacement brand. Tone is warm, plain and lightly witty. Avoid recruitment promises, fabricated activity, patronizing motivation and claims of untraceability or end-to-end encryption.

Primary lockup is horizontal: original offset conversation/peak mark + Space Grotesk full wordmark. Assets: `public/brand/wordmark-light.svg`, `wordmark-dark.svg`, `wordmark-mono.svg`. Text is outlined for portable rendering. The compact mark is reserved for favicons, app icons and small decorative uses; navigation still supplies the full accessible name.

Keep at least half the mark width clear around the lockup. Do not stretch, rotate the complete logo, recolor it outside approved variants, place it on busy imagery, or shorten it to initials. At narrow widths reduce size while keeping the full name readable. Mark minimum 24px; horizontal lockup minimum approximately 190px in ordinary navigation.

Light uses cobalt on warm white; dark uses light cobalt with warm-white lettering. Mono is available for one-color output. Favicon, 512px app icon and 1200×630 social image are in `public/brand` (favicon in `public`). The social line is “The group chat between jobs.” Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-brand.mjs` after preparing the local font as described by the existing brand script. `docs/brand/concepts.svg` records the original mark exploration; it is not a UI screenshot.

Do not imply the mark is legally trademark-cleared. Fonts retain their upstream licenses in `public/fonts`.
